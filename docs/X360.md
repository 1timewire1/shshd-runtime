# The cancelled Xbox 360 game

Two prototypes of a cancelled *Surviving High School* for Xbox 360 survive: **MS3** (April 27, 2009) and **MS4** (May 26, 2009). They're 3D, built by Wanako Games on its
own Tongas engine. This fork translates their stories into `.exp` episodes on the SHS presentation, with the real minigames ported, and uses their art to reskin New Rules'
arena.

Neither prototype's code runs here. No prototype file ships with the runtime: `tools/translate` reads the player's files, plus shots and renders made from them.

**In the library:** four stories, pack 31400, tagged X360 and filed under "Xbox 360", all needing the [`shsx360` addon](ADDONS.md#shsx360-the-cancelled-xbox-360-game).

| # | Story | Source | Minigames |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Day of Diss** | `kingofdiss/*`, MS4 | None. Branching talks with four people, then the battle's intro |
| 2 | **Puzzle Test** | `prototype/puzzle/*` | The jewel puzzle between its two scenes |
| 3 | **Dating Beth** | `episode1/*` | The slot machine, the leash game |
| 4 | **Monday Planner** | `episode3/monday_*`, MS4 | Diss Practice, three times; a speed diss |

## The prototypes

**`data.pak`** is `tongas_pack_v20000`, little-endian:

| Offset | Field |
| --- | --- |
| 0x00 | `char[18]` magic |
| 0x12 | `u32` file count (MS3 624, MS4 1,029) |
| 0x16 | `u32` byte size of the file table, at 0x1A |
| entry | `u32` offset, `u32` stored size, `u32` name length, then the path |

Every blob is an LZMA "alone" stream. There's no encryption.

**What's inside:**
- **Characters** are Cal3D models: `.cfg`, `.csf` skeletons, `.cmf` meshes, `.caf` animations, `.mat.txt` materials.
- **Logic** is Lua 5.1, as source, in `data/scripts` and embedded in the editor templates and maps (`templates/*.wxr`, `maps/*.wxm`).
- **Text** is in `templates/unistrings.wxr`. Each string is `WC` plus 3 digits per UTF-16 unit, low digit first, alphabet `A–Z a–z 0–9 * +`. Codes: `&:R G B;` colour, `!:N;`
  size, `[:id]` includes, `{:image …}` icons.
- **Fonts** are AngelCode BMFont.

**MS3 and MS4 differ** in staging, sound and games, not writing: all 694 of MS3's strings are in MS4 word for word, in the same order, with the same speakers. MS4 adds Day of
Diss, the Diss Battle choice and event, and `hud/choice`. In MS3, `josh` wears Zack's model; MS4 gives him his own and keeps MS3's Kim, Ryan and Hector as "old" models.
MS4 deleted Dating Beth's slot machine functions, so its rules exist only in MS3. MS4 also rebuilt the parking lot for Day of Diss, but the Planner's lot scenes still name the old
map's cameras, so the old lot is `maps/backup/school_parking_lot.wxm`.

## Building

```sh
python tools/translate/translate.py x360 DATA_PAK --story addon --pud RES_LARGE_PUD --x1i X1I_JAR --looks NR_LOOKS_JSON \
    --portraits PORTRAIT_CATALOG --backgrounds BACKGROUNDS --dbp DECK_SHOTS --puzzle PUZZLE_SHOTS --out ADDON_DIR
python tools/translate/translate.py x360 DATA_PAK --story all --addon ADDON_DIR/X360_Addon_Xbox_360.exp ... --out OUT_DIR
```

Each story also writes an editable creator project (`.expproj`). `tools/translate/x360_check.py STORY.expproj --runs N` plays a story in the real `Session` down random paths.

The shots, renders and backgrounds come from tools kept outside the repository, since they run against the prototypes: a Cal3D renderer, and "booths" that patch a prototype to
shoot its art in the Xenia emulator.

## How a story is translated

The logic ships as Lua, so `lua_subset.py` parses it and `x360.py` walks it into creator nodes, with no decompiling.

| Lua | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `OpenDialog(name, n)`, `DialogText(id)` | A line; `OpenDialog("", 8)` is narration; type 6, a sung line, wraps in `{ … {`, SHS's music notes |
| `gameSHS.AddChoices`, `OpenChoice` | A choice; one inside `if flag then` becomes the option's enable condition |
| `gameSHS.SetCharacterEmotion(who, e)` | The expression of that character's later lines (`EMOTION`) |
| `gameStats.x = …` | Set nodes; conditions over them become condition nodes |
| `RunScript`, `game.RunEvent(e)` | Inlined, or a jump to the event |
| `hud/selection/main` | A hub choice, each person enabled while `minSel <= n <= maxSel` |
| `hud/gameover/main` | The message, then the end of the episode |
| Cameras, 3D animation, waits | Dropped |

Day of Diss adds an "Objective Complete" notice with the X360's sound, a location-change transition into the battle, and a tally before the end card (`TALLY_TEXT`).

**One ID per render and background.** `x360.RENDERS` and `x360.SCENES` give each one ID in every story, so the addon holds it once. New entries go at the end. A story declares what
it needs with `Story.addon_revision`, so a later addon needn't rebuild it.

### Art

**Portraits** are rendered offline from the Cal3D models to 226 × 256, with a half-size copy, in one shared pose per character so that only the face changes. Spike is Andy's model,
which nothing else in the prototype uses (`renders={'spike': 'andy'}`). Ryan's Mom and Mrs. Faulkner are the prototype's grey placeholders, and Spud and Dexter are placeholders too.

**Backgrounds** are shot in Xenia at 2560 × 1440 at the prototype's own cameras, one per location, and composed to the frame (`scene_backgrounds`).

**Emoticons.** The prototype's `CreateFx("hud/emoticon/…")` becomes this fork's overlay on the portrait (`TRANSLATED_PORTRAIT_EFFECTS`; see [UI_FIDELITY.md](UI_FIDELITY.md)). The
translator sets global 31410 around a decorated line. Effects on anyone but the speaker are skipped, as only the speaker's portrait is on screen. The cast's SHS relationship badges are
hidden.

### Music

SHS has one music stream, and the X360 layers a level's music and ambience, so each combination is one track, mixed by the translator (`MIXES`, `mix_track`).

- **Level:** a track's gain comes from its music alone, up to RMS 0.15 or peak 0.89. Crowds sit 16 dB under the music, by the owner's ear; the X360's Volumes drowned it out.
- **Day of Diss's theme** plays from intro into loop (`TRANSLATED_MUSIC_LOOPS`, `MusicStream.chain`). No SHS episode joins two tracks that way, so this is a runtime addition. The
  intro is levelled down to the loop's loudness (`even_level`). The iOS host still plays only the intro.
- **Diss Practice's rounds** each have their own `dbp_bg_music` track.
- **`zip.wav`** plays at each dialogue's close, as the X360's `sound/bubble`.

### The slot machine (Dating Beth)

Five slots fill in turn. The one in focus cycles bulb, cross, question, every 500 / 1.4^(slot − 1) ms. A press takes what shows: a bulb scores, a cross doesn't, and both move on; a question
scores nothing and stays. 15 s on the clock. Rules from MS3's `common_functions.lua`.

**Changed from MS3 (owner):**
- A cross is worth 0, not −1, since five slots at ±1 could never total the script's 2.
- 5 bulbs is the best, 3–4 the middle, 2 or fewer fails, as the timed choice scores.
- The clock waits behind `Press OK to start`. Presses during the 0.5 s rise do nothing.

**Kept:** a press resets the option while the period's wait runs, so the next slot opens on the cross.

### The leash game (Dating Beth)

The level starts at 50: 100 wins, 0 loses. A frame with a shake adds 7, and any other loses 12 per second. The wanted shake starts on left-right and alternates with up-down, at an interval of 2–5 s
picked once per game. A shake on the other axis scores nothing. The rules are the same in MS3 and MS4.

**A shake** is a new direction on the same axis, past 0.75 of the stick's travel, within 0.1 s of the last.

**Inputs,** all through one rule (`shake`): a controller's stick at 0.1 s; the D-pad, arrows and WASD at 0.25 s (`SHAKE_WINDOW_MS`); a held mouse button or finger dragging 70 units, at 0.25 s.

**Added:** `Press OK to start`, a line saying what to do, and a 0.3 s fade. **Left out:** the 3D animations on Zack and the dog.

### The jewel puzzle (Puzzle Test)

MS4's `puzzlegame` Lua, line for line where a line decides anything. An 8 × 8 board of five jewels; a held button drags a whole row or column, wrapping.

- **4 or more joined** are selected and go after 3 s, or 1 s during a chain. The comment says 3; the code says 4.
- +1 a jewel, +3 during a chain.
- Jewels fall 0.2 s after a clearing, new ones come in from the top, and a chain lasts 2 s, with the board turning metal.
- 60 s.

**MS4 leaves it unfinished,** so these are this fork's: `Press OK to start`; `Time's Up!` while what's selected clears, then `Your score: N`; the score and clock on screen, in Diss Practice's digits;
`move.wav` for each drag and `move3.wav` for a clearing; and the glow, sparks and metal sweep drawn in 2D. The score goes nowhere, as the story reads none.

**Inputs:** a controller's D-pad or stick, with confirm held to drag; arrows or WASD, with Enter or Space held; a mouse or finger grabs a jewel, its row or column following a cell at a time.

**The art** is additive glass with a scrolling reflection under bloom, so each jewel's 60 frames come from inside the running game, with the shine slowed five times to get smooth frames from a capture.

### Diss Practice (Monday Planner)

MS4's `dissbattlepractice` Lua, line for line where a line decides anything, at 60 frames a second, whatever the player draws. Eight notes ride a record past a tone arm.

- **Notes:** note, broken note and remix arrow, a third each, re-rolled while over the caps of 6 broken and 3 remix.
- **Good hit:** score += 10 × streak × tier, the record speeds up (velocity +10, 30 → 300), streak +1.
- **Tiers:** velocity ≥ 130 (10 straight hits) brings double notes; a streak of 20 brings on-fire notes.
- **Broken note:** streak, tier and speed reset.
- **Remix arrow:** good and broken swap, and the record reverses.
- **A press** swings the arm through `wheel_push.caf` at the trigger's `AnimSpeed` of 4, so PUSH's 967 ms play in 242. A note is caught as its box enters the arm head's, and the head reaches the ring at about 55 ms.
- **60 s.** Over 5,000 passes, and the level goes up.

**Bugs in the Lua, kept on purpose** (owner), so it plays as the X360's does. They're noted in `x360_diss.py`; please don't fix them:
- The good-note cap never counts, as `goodNotesTypeCount` only goes up in a swap.
- A swap makes the speed negative, so `Velocity >= 130` can't bring double notes until the next swap. On-fire notes still come at a streak of 20.
- `SECONDS_TO_REBORN` is 3, but a caught note's first second counts at once, on a clock left from before, so it comes back after about 2 s.

**Changed:** it waits behind a press to start, where MS4 waits behind nothing but its countdown.

**With the switch off,** the rounds are King of Diss's 30 s word game, with goals of 8, 10 and 13 at levels 1–3 (`PRACTICE_TARGETS`).

**Art.** The deck's pieces, digits and notes are shot in Xenia over two flat colours and cut by triangulation matting. The record turns under a light that stays still: `record_base` is the median of eight turns, and the seam lines turn over
it. The notes keep facing the camera, as the X360's do, sized by the fitted camera's depth. `desktop_x360_deck.py` holds what this and the arena's look share.

#### Josh's dance

Josh dances on the steps behind the deck, as frames rendered offline from `DANCE_01` at 15 fps (`x360_dbpdance.py`, kept outside the repository). The frames are drawn one to one under the deck, from one atlas per build.

- **Timing:** the first practice has him stand until 2 s into the countdown (`Wait(2)` in `practice_diss_001`); the others start at once. Whether it's the first goes by the tutorial being due, which only differs if the player skipped the morning's
  practice. Only the drawing depends on it (`_dance_ms`), so saves don't.
- **Rooms:** the hallway's and Josh's room's own marks fall off this frame's deck, so he stays on the same side in all three, at the size each background needs (`dance.places`, keyed by background: hallway 1.15, his room 1.7, with its own feet position). Those two are stretched, so they're a little soft.
- **The loop** jumps where it wraps, as the game does.
- **A placed model must flip x.** Max is right-handed and the world left-handed, and without the flip a character comes out mirrored: side-on it hardly shows, but MS3's dance turned him away from the camera instead of towards it.

The Monday Planner's **Diss Battle** is a speed diss against Spike, as New Rules' translation plays its battle: ten hits to finish, three misses to lose, seven to win. The choice makes the prototype's unused losing lines reachable.

## MS3 Characters

**Options › Add-Ons › MS3 Characters**, off by default, swaps the April build's looks into all four stories. It's portraits and the dancer only; backgrounds, music, rules and text stay as they are.

| Character | With the switch |
| --- | --- |
| Josh | Zack's MS3 look, as MS3 had it |
| Zack | His red MS3 jacket |
| Kim, Ryan, Hector | MS3's looks, from MS4's "old" models |
| Everyone else | No change |

All keep MS4's standard pose, so only the face changes between expressions. MS4's `old ryan` and `old hector` keep MS3's textures beside them, but their materials name the current models' shaders, so a renderer must prefer the texture beside the model.

**The dancer** becomes Zack, doing MS3's own `DANCE_01`: an upright 11.7 s dance, not MS4's breakdance, though it has the same name. MS3's mark, camera and sun are MS4's, and only the morning's ambient differs (0.50 to 0.46). MS4's timing stays.

**How it works:** the set is in the addon, 2000 above the usual IDs (`x360.MS3_LOOKS`, the profile's `MS3_CHARACTER_ART`). `EpisodeResources._hires_portrait` tries that ID first and falls back to the usual one, so anyone outside the set stays as they are, and so does an older addon. Saves name the
standard portraits, so they don't change, and it takes effect at once.

## The X360 Arena Look

With the addon imported, **X360 Arena Look** (Options › Add-Ons, off by default) draws [New Rules' arena](NEW_RULES.md#the-arena) on the X360's turntable. `X360ArenaRenderer` subclasses New Rules' renderer, so the panel's geometry, click targets, rules and saves are the same, and New Rules' stories need no rebuild. It reads the skin straight from the addon (`library_has_skin`).

| Part | New Rules | X360 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backdrop | The frame | The title screen's layers, with moving clouds and glints |
| Ring | A yellow disc | The record's top face, items riding its rim |
| Selector | A trapezoid | The tone arm's needle; the arm swings on presses in play, at the X360's 242 ms |
| Notes | New Rules' items | Diss Practice's notes, at its depth, with its smoke, sparks and flames (`ITEM_NOTES`). The top-speed note is the double note in flames |
| Other items | New Rules' pictures | Those with an ink outline and shadow, or the X360's own for crosses and stamps |
| Score, time | Dials | The 3D digits |
| Meter | A yellow arc | The power bar, with triangle markers |
| Labels | Bitmap fonts | Red and white Badaboom |
| "correct!", "wrong!" | Text | The YEAH! and OOOPS! stamps |
| Bubbles | New Rules' | The X360's, nine-sliced; tutorials carry Diss Practice's cards |
| Sounds | Tracks 13, 1, 11 | `whoosh_loud`, `dbp_fail`, `dbp_swap`, only while the arena is open (`Desktop._x360_swaps`) |
| Music | Track 14 | `dbp_bg_music`, fading at "Time's up!" |

A 3–2–1 countdown is decoration after "press ok to start". Here, hits are still decided by the slot under the selector, as New Rules decides them; the arm is only drawn. A damaged addon falls back to New Rules' look.

## Known limits

- The leash and the puzzle haven't been played on a real controller or touch screen; the tests feed in events from `gamepad.py`'s shapes.
- The leash's on-screen joystick animates at an inferred 0.125 s a cell. Which stick the prototype reads isn't named in the decompilation; ours is the left.
- Zack's trousers in MS3's dance read slightly less olive than in the prototype, as the outdoor ambient's green tint isn't applied: one set of frames serves all three rooms.
- The iOS host doesn't yet chain Day of Diss's intro into its loop. Sync Devices doesn't send addons.
